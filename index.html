What it does

Pricing that holds up in front of a customer

Every figure on screen, in the emailed proposal and in the slide deck comes from one shared calculation — so the numbers can never drift apart between them.

Compare terms side by side

Put monthly, yearly and multi-year deals in adjacent rows and change one variable at a time — users, plan, hours, hosting or discount — without disturbing the others.

Honest year-by-year cash

Year 1 carries implementation and the undiscounted first-year licence, so an averaged monthly figure misleads. The real schedule is shown year by year.

Present without leaving the page

Turn the quote into a slide deck built from the official Odoo pricing deck, pick the slides you want, and present it full screen or windowed in the browser.

Send it as a real proposal

One click composes a branded pricing proposal — totals, savings, year-by-year schedule and payment options — and sends it from the advisor's own Gmail account.

Internal figures stay internal

Commission and margin views live entirely outside the customer-facing calculation, so they cannot reach an emailed proposal or a presented slide.

Export a clean PDF

Print or export the customer-facing quote as a tidy PDF, with the working controls and any internal view stripped out of the output.

How it works

Three steps, one page

Set the user count, then add a row for each shape of deal you want to compare. Licence, implementation packs, hosting and discounts all recalculate as you type.

Data & privacy

What this app accesses, and why

The calculator is a static web page with no application server and no database. It runs entirely in the browser, and the only external service it talks to is Gmail — and only when an advisor chooses to send a quote.

Google account access

Signing in with Google is optional and is only needed to email a quote. If you never send one, the app never asks for a Google account at all.

  • Sending one email on your behalf. When you press Send, the finished proposal is sent from your own Gmail address so the customer receives it from their advisor, not from a no-reply robot.
  • Your email address. Read once, purely so the app can show which account is signed in and set the message's From address.
  • No mailbox access, ever. The app cannot read, search, modify, label or delete any message in your mailbox, and it does not request the permissions that would allow it to.
  • Nothing is stored or shared. Quotes, recipients and message text are never sent to a server of ours, never written to a database, and never shared with anyone. The access token is held in memory only and is discarded when you sign out or close the tab.
Requested scope

gmail.send — the narrowest Gmail scope that exists. It permits sending a message and nothing else: it grants no ability to read or even list the contents of a mailbox. Alongside it, userinfo.email supplies just the signed-in address.

Full details are in the privacy policy. Google's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
