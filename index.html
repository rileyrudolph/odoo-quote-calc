The tool

What it actually does

Built to get you from "so what would it cost?" to a sent proposal without leaving the page.

Line the options up next to each other

Monthly, yearly, three-year — one row each. Change the users, plan, hours, hosting or discount on one row and the others stay exactly where they were.

Answer "what do I pay in year three?"

Year 1 carries the implementation and the full-price licence, so an averaged monthly number is misleading. You get the honest year-by-year instead.

Send a proposal that looks the part

Totals, savings, the year-by-year and payment options, laid out properly and sent from your own Gmail so it lands as coming from you. Preview it before it goes.

Present it without switching apps

The pricing deck is right there with your numbers dropped into it. Pick your slides and present full screen, or keep the browser bar if you're sharing a tab.

Your commission stays yours

The internal view is worked out separately from the customer numbers, so there's no way for it to end up in an email or on a slide by accident.
