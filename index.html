Why people move to Odoo

One platform, not seven tools

Your quotes, stock, invoices and customers live in the same system, so nothing has to be re-keyed or reconciled between apps.

Priced to actually fit

One price per user with every app included — no per-module surprises, and no enterprise licence that dwarfs the problem you're solving.

Open source

The code is open and the community is enormous. You're never locked in, and there's almost always someone who has solved your edge case already.

Start with one app. Add users, add modules, add companies — the system scales without a replatform every few years.

Try it before you buy it

There's a free trial and a free plan, so you can put it in front of the team and see whether it fits before anyone signs anything.

Upgrades and support included

Hosting, upgrades, bug fixes and support come with the subscription rather than arriving as a separate line item every year.
