How to play

Pick a stake, then press Deal. You get a customer and three possible concessions. Each one makes them more likely to sign, but costs you money. Take as few as you can get away with, then press Close. Go past 21 and you have given away too much and lost the deal. Bigger stakes pay more but bring tougher customers.

What to do

The best move in every situation, worked out exactly for your settings. Down the side is your total so far. Across the top is the customer's first card. This is the average customer though: the real one in front of you may be tougher or softer, and you are never told which.

Close Concede
Why it says that

It is chasing money kept, not deals won. Every concession you take is money off the invoice, so the chart tells you to stop earlier than a normal blackjack chart would. Closing on 17 pays a bonus that closing on 20 does not — but only if you actually beat them. If the customer talks themselves out of it you still keep the deal, just at flat rate, because you did not win that one.
