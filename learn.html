Reading it

The chart maximises banked MRR, not close rate. That is why it tells you to stand earlier than a blackjack chart would: closing on 17 pays a multiple that closing on 20 does not, so a slightly worse chance at a much larger cheque is often the better play. This is the whole argument for holding your discount, in one grid.
