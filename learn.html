How to play

Choose what to risk, then press Deal.

Every customer is worth a monthly amount, shown as Worth. They open by asking for something.

Give in hands them their ask. It moves them a long way, and it costs you the most.

Counter offers three cheaper things instead. Each one moves them less.

Close signs the deal. You keep whatever you did not give away.

Go past 21 and the deal dies, and everything you conceded dies with it.

Nickel and diming will not get you there on its own. Sooner or later you have to give them something real.

Closing early pays a bonus, but only when you beat them outright. If they talk themselves out of it, you keep the deal at flat rate.

Bigger wagers pay more, and bring tougher customers.
