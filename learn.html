How to play

You are building a quote. Every deal starts small and your job is to make it bigger without losing it.

Grow the quote adds seats, term, a Success Pack or Odoo.sh. Every one of those is worth real money every month, and every one asks a bit more of the customer.

Ask more than they will carry and they walk. You get nothing, and everything you built goes with them. The number to watch is how much you have asked, out of 21.

Send the quote when you think they will still sign. Whatever they sign is what you bank.

About discounting

A discount does not make the quote better. It buys you room with somebody who has run out of patience, and it costs you every month for the length of the term.

You get one licence discount and one Success Pack discount per quote, and the licence one has a ceiling that depends on the term.

Every discount card prints what it buys before you spend it. Most customers give a point or two back. Somebody who has threatened to walk since day one gives nothing at all, because they were always going to ask again.

Discounting every hand lifts your close rate and lowers what you are paid. The board ranks your best month of commission, so the best scores come from long terms closed at full price.

How to play

You are the rep. Every hand is one live deal, and your job is the same as it is at work: build a quote that actually covers what the customer needs, then send it.

The two sides

Their side is a stack of objections. One is face up, the rest are not, and together they add up to a number between 16 and 21. Your side is your quote, and what it asks of them is the number on the table. Beat their number without going past 21 and they sign.

The buttons

  • Deal. Next customer.
  • Add to the quote. Deal yourself the growth cards. Every one is worth real money and pushes your number up. Looking is free: click the button again, or press Esc, to close the table without taking anything.
  • Give a discount. Every objection on their side is worth points. A discount subtracts points from the ones about money and nothing from the rest, so their total drops and yours stays put. Costs you margin every month of the term.
  • Double down. One more card, double stakes. The quote sends itself straight after, win or lose.
  • Send the quote. Stop and find out.

The cards

Each card prints what it adds and what it costs you: more seats, a longer term, a Success Pack, Odoo.sh, the Custom plan. The money is what lands on your book. The ask is how much further it pushes you toward 21, printed as a band because the exact price moves a little, the way a real customer does.

The chip

The chip is the size of room you walk into. Bigger chip, bigger customer, worth far more if it lands and more expensive to chase if it does not.

How a hand ends

  • Signed. You beat their number and stayed at or under 21.
  • Stalled. You matched it exactly. Nothing banked, nothing lost, and it rolls to next quarter. About a quarter of hands.
  • Not enough. You came up short and they went with somebody who scoped it properly. You carry the pursuit cost.
  • They walked. You asked for more than this one would carry. Everything you built is gone, and it burns two slots of the month instead of one. Where they stop is not the same for everybody, so this one is avoidable.

About discounts

A discount rescues about one deal in four that would otherwise have been lost, and it costs you book value every time you give one. That trade is the whole point: it lifts your close rate and lowers what you banked, which is why the board ranks book value.

It only subtracts from objections that were about money. Their card says “Budget was set last quarter” and a discount takes points off it. It says “Has questions about data migration” and a discount takes nothing off, because money was never the problem. Each discount card prints how many points it buys back before you spend anything, and against somebody who has threatened to walk since day one it says plainly: nothing.

The rules you cannot break

The quote can only do what an Odoo rep can actually do. Multi-year licence discounts have a ceiling by term, one-year deals get none of it, implementation discounts stop at 30 percent, hosting stops at eight workers before it needs a dedicated server, and you cannot take the same concession twice. The Rules tab in the Menu lists all of them.
