# Pricing Menuing — 10 Minutes
**DS Training: Advanced Pricing · Fri Aug 28, 2:00pm · Buffalo-10-Studio**
Scenario: 5-user company. SOW done. No terms conversation yet. Live demo tool: **2024 Pricing Calculator** (North America, Custom plan).

---

## The one idea

Don't quote a price — present a menu. "Yes or no" becomes "which one," and you decide what's on it.

Two axes to set at once, together, not separately:
1. **Implementation** — 50-hr self-implementation vs. full (the SOW)
2. **Term** — and we want them to land on **5 years**

---

| Path | Hours | List rate | List cost | At max 30% off | Discounted cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Self-implementation | 50 hrs | $140/hr | $7,000 | 30% | $4,900 |
| Full (SOW, ≤100 hrs) | *[fill in]* | $125–140/hr | *[hrs × rate]* | 30% | *[× 0.70]* |

**Say this explicitly:** "Two separate discounts, two separate levers. The licensing discount comes from the term — five years gets you 15% off, automatically. The implementation discount is mine to offer — up to 30% off the first hundred hours. I can move the second one to help you move on the first."

That's the actual close mechanic: **trade implementation discount for term length.** You're not discounting the license to win the deal — you're discounting the hours, which costs less and buys the same commitment.

**The line to say:** "Licensing is nearly flat across every term — thirty-seven to thirty-nine dollars a user, no matter which you pick. The five-year isn't more expensive per month. It's the only one where that rate is locked for five years instead of one."

## Run of show

**0:00–1:00 · Frame it**
"Five users, SOW's done, we haven't talked terms. That's not a gap — that's the conversation. Terms are the product, not the afterthought."

**1:00–4:00 · Open the calculator, live**
Set users = 5, plan = Custom, North America. Walk the term columns left to right, read the monthly numbers out loud. Let them see the discount stack in real time (5% → 10% → 13% → 15%).

**4:00–7:00 · Make the case for full-implementation + 5-year**

1. **Rate lock.** $38.11/user/month locked for 60 months vs. re-quoting every year.
2. **Discount compounds with term, not with implementation choice** — so the term decision and the implementation decision aren't in tension. Pick both long.
3. **Full implementation removes the self-implementation risk.** 50 hours is enough to install it. It's not enough to get it adopted — and adoption is what makes the 5-year rate lock worth something.

**7:00–9:00 · Objections**

> *"Can we self-implement to save money now?"*
> "You can — 50 hours at $140. It saves upfront, but you're the one running config, testing, and training. Before you go that route though: I can take up to 30% off the first 100 hours of the full implementation. That often closes most of the gap without you taking on the risk."

> *"Why commit to 5 years before we've even talked terms?"*
> "Because the calculator already shows you the answer — the license rate barely moves month to month across any term. The only thing that changes is how long you get to keep it."

**9:00–10:00 · Close**
"Show the menu, not the number. Recommend full implementation and 5 years — say why — then stop talking and let them respond."
